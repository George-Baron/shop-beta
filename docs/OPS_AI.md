# VELTO AI Operations Manager (Phase 1: Command Centre, Phase 2: Procurement, Phase 3: Global order management, Phase 4: Logistics, Phase 5: Import / export, Phase 6: Insights and reports)

Open **Admin > AI Operations** (`/admin#/opsai`). Staff need the new **AI Operations** permission (role "Operations Supervisor" has it); the owner always does.

## What it does today
- **Command Centre**: live figures from the real tables (orders by status and channel, retail stock, the separate wholesale warehouse, riders, suppliers, import/export, returns, approvals, exceptions). Costs the system does not record are listed as "not recorded", never estimated.
- **Assistant**: natural-language instructions. It reads data with `read_data` and acts only through `take_action`. Under each reply the screen shows the status the *backend* returned (Done / Recommended / Waiting for approval / Blocked / Failed). Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (model: `OPS_ASSISTANT_MODEL`); without it you get a live summary.
- **Monitor** (worker job `cron.opsMonitor`, every 5 min): raises one exception per problem (late orders, orders waiting for dispatch, slow dropship suppliers, sales below cost, out-of-stock sellers, customs holds, past-ETA shipments, overdue supplier payables, stale GPS, failed jobs, approvals waiting) and closes them when they clear. It only observes.
- **Approvals**, **Exceptions**, **Audit log**, **Settings & agents** (mode, department modes, limits, 10 agents on/off, approvers, connection status).

## Control model
- Modes: **Monitor** (default: recommend only), **Assisted** (a person approves each action), **Controlled** (routine level-1 actions run alone inside a daily budget). The overall mode is a ceiling; a department can be set lower, never higher. Financial is capped at Assisted.
- Levels 0-4 with the starting limits 100k / 500k / 2M (configurable per category). Amounts to the same counterparty inside the window are added together, so splitting cannot dodge a limit. A foreign amount with no recorded exchange rate is "value unknown" and treated as level 4.
- From level 4, two different people must approve. Nobody approves their own request or decides twice. Approvals are bound to a hash of the exact terms; any later change invalidates them.
- The AI can never pay, transfer, change bank details, or approve. Money steps are carried out by a person, who records the reference.
- Segregation of duties is recorded per purchase (request, approve, receive, verify, pay).
- Actions with no system behind them yet are refused, not faked.
- The audit table `ops_actions` is append-only (database trigger); it is also protected during backup restore.

## Phase 2: procurement intelligence (AI Operations > Procurement)
- **Requests**: "Create requests from real stock" works out what to reorder from stock, incoming orders and 30-day sales (retail), and from reorder levels (wholesale). Retail and wholesale are never added together. The numbers behind each request are saved with it. One open request per product and destination.
- **Vendors**: every vendor starts *under review* with nothing verified. Only a person can mark a check verified (and must say how). Approving or preferring a vendor needs verified registration **and** contact; losing either drops an approved vendor to *conditional*. A person with approver level 2+ changes a status directly; anyone else, and the AI, must file a request for an approver.
- **Quotations** are prices vendors actually gave us. Each needs a source and, for vendor quotes, a reference. The AI never writes a price.
- **Comparison**: landed cost in Naira (goods, freight, insurance, duty, VAT, clearing; duty/VAT are marked as estimates), then a 0-100 score from the eight weighted factors (weights editable in Settings). Only factors with real data count, and each score says how much of the weight that is. Cheapest, fastest, preferred and recommended are shown with the reason. Only approved/preferred/conditional vendors on valid quotes that cover the quantity can be recommended; a quotation whose Naira cost is unknown (no exchange rate) is not ranked. Fewer than 3 qualified quotes is flagged.
- **Purchase orders**: drafting commits nothing. "Request approval" goes through the normal rules (level by value, split detection, two-person, requester cannot approve). The approval is bound to the PO's exact terms; any change after submission makes it fail. PDF export. A person records "sent" and "goods received" (the AI cannot); late or short deliveries feed the vendor's delivery score and raise an exception. Recording receipt does **not** change stock: receive it in the warehouse as usual.
- Still not connected: supplier **discovery** (no research source is wired, so the AI says so instead of inventing vendors), emailing vendors, courier APIs.

## Phase 3: every order, followed until it ends (AI Operations > Orders)
- A sweep runs every 2 minutes (`cron.opsOrders`) and registers every open retail, partner, dropshipping, international and wholesale order the first time it appears (`ops_order_watch`, history in `ops_order_stage_events`).
- Each order is placed in a stage from the store's own records (paid, awaiting dispatch, rider offered, out for delivery, awaiting supplier, wholesale picking/packed/dispatched, failed, under investigation) with the party who must act next.
- When a stage runs past its allowance (configurable, `order_sla`) one exception is raised with the recommended action. Orders waiting on the customer (payment, pickup) are shown but never counted late.
- Routine follow-up goes through the gateway so it is logged and obeys modes and permissions: an unassigned ready order is offered to the best rider (`rider.assign`), other late orders get a drafted customer update. Retries back off (15 min, doubling) and stop after 5; the exception is then raised to high and says the AI has stopped.
- An order closes only when the store itself records delivery, cancellation or refund. Delivery is labelled **verified** (OTP, photo or recipient confirmation; wholesale signer), **pickup**, **supplier reported** or **unproven**; unproven deliveries raise an exception for a person. Estimates and unverified messages never close an order.
- The sweep reads and writes only its own tables; it never edits an order, stock or money. Settings: `orders_follow_up` (nudges on/off), `order_sla`, the Order Management Agent switch.
- Limits: the store does not record web vs app, so both show as retail; partner fulfilment confirmation and stock shortage per order are not tracked by the store, so those stages are not claimed.

## Phase 4: logistics (AI Operations > Logistics)
- **Providers** (courier, third party, freight, cargo, independent rider) start "under review"; only the owner approves or suspends, with a reason. Nothing is approved by being added.
- **Rates** are only what a person recorded from a real quotation, contract or published rate card, with a reference and optional expiry. Expired, wrong-destination, overweight, unapproved and unconvertible (no verified exchange rate) options are listed with the reason and never compared.
- **Comparison** (`/logistics/compare`, assistant tool `courier_compare`): scores reliability, speed, cost and safety with weights you can set (`delivery_weights`). Reliability and safety come from your own finished shipments and need 5 before they count. Providers with missing data are ranked down so an unknown cannot beat a proven provider. The explanation says what was and was not known. Own riders are not priced here; rider dispatch handles them.
- **Booking** goes through the gateway (`courier.book_local`, level 1 by amount; `courier.book_intl`, level 3). Because no courier API is connected, the AI only creates a booking REQUEST and a task; a person books with the courier and enters the tracking reference, which is when it becomes "booked". An order cannot have two open shipments, and a rate that changed after approval voids the approval.
- **Tracking** is what staff record from the courier. Moves must follow a sensible order. "Delivered" needs proof (courier confirmation, OTP, signature, photo or customer confirmation with a reference); without it the shipment is only "delivered claimed" and an exception is raised.
- **Watch** raises exceptions for unbooked requests, uncollected parcels, no tracking update for 24 h, past ETA, failed deliveries and unproven deliveries.
- **Track record** per provider (delivered %, on time %, average hours and cost) with a plain verdict; an underperformer is flagged for a person to decide.
- DHL, UPS and FedEx still show as **not connected**; they will only be marked connected after credentials and a test succeed.

## Phase 5: import and export (AI Operations > Import / export)
- The consignments, documents, permit reminders and events stay in the store's own Import & export module. The AI layer reads them, checks readiness and links them to orders and purchase orders (`ops_trade_links`); it adds no second trade record.
- **Readiness** (`lib/ops/trade-readiness.js`) says what is missing for the next step: named party, goods with HS code, value and origin, commercial invoice and packing list, transport document before booking, forwarder, expired ETA, customs query or hold, unverified supplier on the linked purchase order, short stock for exports, and buyer address/country for exports. Landed cost is labelled an ESTIMATE and its gaps (freight, duty, agent fee, exchange rate) are named; nothing is guessed.
- **Compliance** is only escalated. Regulated classes and unticked required permit reminders block the consignment and raise an exception for compliance. The AI never ticks a reminder, never classifies goods, and never moves a consignment forward.
- **Orders** linked to a consignment follow it (stage "international shipment in progress") and are never offered to a local rider.
- **Import procurement**: an approved purchase order for a foreign supplier can have a DRAFT consignment created (`consignment.draft`, level 1, approval in Assisted mode). Incoterm, transport and costs are left unconfirmed and the note says so. One consignment per purchase order.
- **Watch**: compliance review needed, unmet requirements once booked, no update for 7 days, incomplete landed cost after arrival. Customs holds and late ETAs are still raised by the main monitor.
- Limits: freight quotations and clearing-agent fees are not yet recorded as quotations (only entered on the shipment); no customs or shipping API is connected; HS classification and duty rates are not looked up.

## Phase 6: advanced intelligence (AI Operations > Insights, Reports)
Everything is computed by code from recorded data. No model writes a figure.
- **Demand forecast** (`lib/ops/forecast.js`): per-product daily rate from real retail sales (dropship excluded, wholesale never mixed in), trend, days of stock, stock-out before restock (counting incoming purchase orders), and a suggested order quantity. With under 14 days of history there is no forecast; with few sales confidence is low and no quantity is suggested. It **measures its own accuracy** by forecasting the last 4 weeks from earlier data and comparing with what sold (needs 56+ days).
- **At-risk open orders**: compares how long an order has sat in a stage with how long past orders took in that stage (p75/p90). A stage with fewer than 10 past orders is not predicted.
- **Supplier scorecards**: purchasing suppliers (fill rate, on-time, cancellations from receipts) and dropship suppliers (delivery success, cancel and issue rates, reply and ship hours). Too little history is not scored; poor results are flagged for a person to decide. Product quality, invoice accuracy and disputes are not recorded anywhere yet and are listed as not tracked.
- **Profitability**: per order from recorded figures only (catalogue cost, supplier cost, commission, courier cost). An order missing any product cost is left out and counted as unknown, never treated as zero cost. Catalogue cost is a standard cost, not necessarily what was paid. Rider pay is not recorded, so delivery cost counts only where a courier shipment recorded it.
- **AI performance**: orders watched and delivered, on-time and proof rates, issues resolved, approval response time, action success (executed vs failed) and human-intervention rate. **Verified** savings (a received purchase priced below the previous received price for the same product) are kept apart from **estimated** opportunity (dearest vs chosen quotation), per currency, never added across currencies.
- **Reports**: daily, weekly and monthly reports are written automatically (the worker checks hourly) once a Lagos-time period has ended, stored unchanged (`ops_reports`) with a plain headline and the full figures, and logged. Make-now buttons exist for the latest of each kind.
- Limits: forecasts ignore seasonality until there is more than a year of history; lead time is assumed 7 days; no rider cost; profitability depends on product costs being filled in.

## Phase 7: controlled autonomy (AI Operations > Go-live)
Autonomy is earned, not switched on. The AI starts in Monitor mode.
- **Rollout gate** (`lib/ops/autonomy.js`): raising a department to Assisted needs two approvers, one at level 3+. Raising it to Controlled also needs: the monitor on and ran in the last 30 min; no open critical exceptions; a passing safeguard test in the last 24 h; at least 10 of its Assisted-mode proposals decided by people with 80%+ approved; the owner's acknowledgement for that department; and, for riders, verified riders with live GPS, for couriers an approved provider with a current rate. The gate is enforced when settings are saved. Only the owner can override, with a written reason (15+ characters), which is logged. Financial can never be overridden: it is capped at Assisted.
- **Safeguard test** (`lib/ops/safeguards.js`): runs every action in every mode at many amounts through the real policy and checks the "AI must never" rules (no payments or banking changes, nothing above level 1 on its own, no self-approval, no splitting to avoid limits, unknown currency treated as highest level, nothing runs while paused or past the daily limit, SoD). The unit tests also break the policy on purpose to prove the test notices.
- **Pause switch**: while paused, only level-0 work (look, draft, tell) runs; everything else asks a person. **Circuit breaker**: if 5 autonomous actions fail within 30 minutes (configurable) autonomy pauses itself and raises a critical exception; a person resumes it. **Daily cap**: past 200 autonomous actions in 24 h (configurable), actions ask a person.
- **Suggested rollout**: Monitor for a few days -> Assisted for everything for 1-2 weeks (decide each proposal) -> run the safeguard test -> accept limits and make Riders Controlled -> after a week add Couriers and Retail -> keep Procurement, Refunds and Import/export in Assisted until you trust them. Financial stays with people.
- Tests: `npm run test:ops-autonomy:unit`, `npm run test:ops-autonomy`.
